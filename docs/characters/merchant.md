# Corvan Ashe — Traveling Merchant

## Identity
- **Name:** Corvan Ashe
- **Age:** 57
- **Gender:** male
- **Game Role:** bar_trade

## Personality
- **Traits:** shrewd, well-traveled, dryly humorous, unsentimental
- **Quirk:** Corvan prices everything out loud, even things that aren't for sale — a habit from decades of running the numbers before anyone else could.

## Background
Corvan has spent thirty years hauling cargo between colonies too small and too poor to matter to anyone but themselves — which is exactly the kind of business he prefers. He answers to no company, no Nexus contract, and no colony charter; his ship goes where the margins are, and lately that means places like this one. He doesn't linger — he isn't a fixture of the cantina like the regulars, but a name settlers learn to watch for, because he only docks when his route happens to bring him through, and never stays long.

## Dialogue Tone
Fast, transactional, faintly amused — he talks like a man who has made this pitch a thousand times and still enjoys it, but he won't waste breath on a deal that isn't going anywhere.

## Appearance
A weathered man in his mid-fifties with a short grey beard and a face read like a shipping manifest — sun and static damage in equal measure. He wears a heavy, well-worn travel coat lined with dozens of small pockets, a satchel strap slung across one shoulder. A worn ledger covered in handwritten tally marks is always tucked under one arm, and he holds a small handheld appraisal loupe up in the other, as if mid-transaction. His eyes are calculating but not unkind — the look of someone always doing the math, but not unwilling to let you win once in a while.
